Privacy Policy for Table Tracker

Last updated: 3/25/2025

Introduction

Thank you for choosing to use Table Tracker ("the App"). This Privacy Policy is designed to help you understand our approach to data and privacy when you use our mobile application.

We respect your privacy and are committed to providing a secure, user-friendly experience with minimal data collection.

Information We Do Not Collect

Table Tracker is designed with privacy in mind. We want to be clear that:

  • We do not collect any personal information
  • We do not collect device information
  • We do not use analytics tools to track your usage
  • We do not store your data on external servers
  • We do not use cookies or tracking technologies

Data Storage

Table Tracker stores all user data (table configurations, occupancy status, and settings) locally on your device only. This data is managed by the iOS system's built-in data protection mechanisms and remains on your device.

We do not have access to this information, and it is not transmitted to us or any third parties.

How Your Data Is Used

Since we don't collect any information from you or your device:

  • We do not analyze or process your data
  • We do not create user profiles
  • We do not target advertisements
  • We do not share data with any third parties

The App functions entirely on your device, using only the local storage necessary for its core functionality.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

Table Tracker does not share any user data with third parties because we don't collect any data to share. We do not sell, rent, or lease any information to any third-party entities.

Your Rights and Choices

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access and Control: All data is stored locally on your device and can be deleted at any time by deleting the App.
  • Delete Your Data: You can clear all App data through your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

Children's Privacy

Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.

App Permissions

Table Tracker requires the following permissions to function properly:

  • Local Storage: To save your table configurations and settings on your device

The App does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or other device features.
